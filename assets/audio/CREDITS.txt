Missile / explosion samples
---------------------------
Layered from Kenney "Sci-Fi Sounds" (CC0 1.0)
https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds
Also mirrored on OpenGameArt.

Sources used:
- thrusterFire_003 + impactMetal_001  -> missile.wav
- explosionCrunch_004 + lowFrequency_explosion_000 + explosionCrunch_001 -> boom.wav
- lowFrequency_explosion_000 + explosionCrunch_002 + slime_000 -> boom_water.wav

Engine / afterburner / roll loops remain project-generated via tools/generate_audio.py.
Rebake missile/boom with tools/bake_combat_sfx.ps1 when FFmpeg is available.
